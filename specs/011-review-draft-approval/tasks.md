## Implementation Strategy

MVP = Phases 1 to 3 (single-PR draft, preview, approval, guarded post). Then US2 (bulk, the mode that triggered the issue), then US3 and US5 (attribution and lint; both are pure script work with tests), US4 (template and rewrite scope), US6 (unattended), Polish.
